About us

A neighborhood tea bar, powered by pedals.

Pedal-Powered Refreshment is a small tea bar concept built around one idea: a good drink makes the scenic route better. We brew milk tea, Yakult drinks, basic tea and fruit tea, always served iced and made your way.

Brewed with care

Every cup starts with freshly brewed tea. You choose the flavor, size, add-ons and sugar level.

Simple, honest prices

Good things start from ₱20. Add-ons are priced per cup, so your estimated total is always clear before you send it.

Ordered over a chat

Build your order here, save the picture, and send it to our Facebook page. We confirm availability, payment and pickup with you.
